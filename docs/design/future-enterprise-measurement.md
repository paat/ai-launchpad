# Future direction: enterprise AI adoption measurement

Status: product direction agreed 27 October 2026; outside the hackathon implementation.

AI Launchpad's service for micro-entrepreneurs remains free. A future paid offering may help organizations with multiple employees understand whether AI adoption is improving their work.

The agreed outcome measures are time saved, reliable outcomes, and remaining manual work. Usage counts alone do not demonstrate business value. Estimated savings must remain distinguishable from observed savings; reliability needs an explicit outcome and evidence; remaining work must include gaps and human responsibilities.

The hackathon implements only the free micro-entrepreneur service. Billing, enterprise administration, employee monitoring and enterprise reporting are not part of this build. Pricing, measurement methodology, consent and organization-level privacy remain future design decisions.
