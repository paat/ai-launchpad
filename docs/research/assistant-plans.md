## UNKNOWN, and the default to use

- **EUR amounts for Claude and ChatGPT.** Pages publish USD before tax, or the Estonia checkout number did not render. Default: store the USD list and `tax=extra`; do not convert. Do not invent €22 or €23.
- **Claude consumer training toggle’s initial position.** Use is conditioned on the user allowing it [5]. The pricing table cell says “Opt-out,” which was not a sentence. Default: treat training as off unless “Help improve Claude” is on.
- **ChatGPT Plus custom remote MCP.** Developer mode is Jah [9]; the MCP article does not grant Plus a server connection [10]. Default: `custom_mcp_connectors=no` and `mcp_write_tools=no` for Plus.
- **ChatGPT Pro Google Drive sync vs gmail.com**, and which plans get Gmail, Calendar, and Outlook. Default: do not assume Free or Go have Gmail, Drive, or Outlook. Treat Business as having Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 [16]. Treat Pro as having Google Drive sync only where the help article says Pro.
- **Numeric custom-connector caps** above Claude Free. Default: no cap, except Team/Enterprise, where an owner must add the connector.
- **Whether €21.99 and Workspace €16.20 include VAT.** Microsoft states ex-VAT [22]. Google does not on the cards fetched. Default: charge the displayed consumer EUR as shown and do not add VAT again; for Workspace, do not assume VAT is inside €16.20.
- **Workspace Business Standard custom MCP, phone, and training.** Default: no custom MCP (work accounts are excluded from Gemini Apps custom MCP [18]); training no, only by analogy with Gemini Enterprise’s “not used to train” line on the same EE subscriptions page, which is a different product.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot month-to-month price, phone MCP, training default, and Copilot chat UI language.** Default: price = €18.20 ex-VAT per user per month on an annual commitment, or €15.60 while the published promo runs (through 31 Dec 2026); custom MCP = admin-only yes [24]; phone = no; training = no; Estonian UI = unknown, do not require it.
- **Consumer Copilot custom MCP, training, and UI language.** Default: no custom MCP, training unknown so do not send business data, UI language not a gate. The leg’s table row cites no URL. The fact sheet points at source 23, the only consumer Copilot page in this memo (Pro purchase ended). The free-row values are this table and these defaults, not new quotes from that page.
- **Meta AI Estonian UI and the standalone app in Estonia.** Default: available through EU Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger [26]; do not require an Estonian UI; training on unless the user has objected [25].
- **Gemini Keep Activity initial state.** Default: treat chats as used for training until Keep Activity is turned off [20].

## What changed versus the in-repo facts

Claude Free’s one-custom-connector limit is now written in Anthropic’s help center, and write tools (create, modify, delete) are allowed there with per-tool approval, which matches the 30–31 Oct 2026 tests. ChatGPT write tools on a custom MCP still need Business or Enterprise/Edu, web only, and an admin or owner must create and publish them; Pro may add read/fetch custom MCP, so the “write tools need Business” rule still holds and should not be widened to Plus or Pro. Claude Max on the current price card is the USD 100 5× plan; the USD 200 tier is named Ultra, and Team standard is USD 25 per seat per month before tax.

- **Custom MCP on the phone vs web add flow.** Launch note puts connectors on Web, iOS, and Android and announces Bring Your Own MCP in the same post [32]. The only add steps are grok.com [31]. Default: the phone can use a connector added on the account.
- **Estonia EUR for Grok tiers.** x.ai/pricing is USD and “do not include applicable taxes or fees (e.g. VAT)” [29]. Play’s Estonia page says VAT is included in store prices but showed no Grok SKU amounts [42]. Default: quote USD ex-VAT and read EUR at Estonian checkout.
- **X Premium tax.** Estonia web amounts are published [39]; the help page did not say whether VAT is inside them. Default: treat them as the web list and confirm tax at X checkout.
- **Estonian UI.** Marketing says “30+ languages” and does not name Estonian [45]. The Play page in Estonian is the store listing [42]. Default: do not claim an Estonian app UI.
- **SuperGrok and Heavy list price.** New buyers are on The X Subscription; old SuperGrok price and features continue [30][33]. Heavy is not priced there [43]. Default: do not invent $30 or $300. These rows stay out of the plan sheet.
- **Whether Teams $30 and “Grok Business” $30 are the same SKU.** Both are published [29][40]. Default: minimum seats 1 until a page states a minimum; match the seat in billing before assuming the admin connector console. `write_tools` stays unstated.
