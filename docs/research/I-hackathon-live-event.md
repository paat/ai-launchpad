## Implications for this project

These are our conclusions from the slides and the [product brief](../../README.md), not statements made by the organizers:

1. Lead the demo with a concrete small-business outcome and time saved. Show one useful workflow end to end.
2. Demonstrate genuine Meta runtime use: for example, screenshot-guided setup and friction classification. Development through a Meta coding assistant should not be used as the sole evidence of product eligibility.
3. Make the MCP gateway, deterministic obligations, exact-action approvals and receipts visible as the substance of the product. A catalogue or dashboard alone would be a weak fit to the displayed guidance.
4. Identify the existing Ärikratt public MCP as an external dependency and clearly separate it from the new event-built work.
5. Partner-hosted Muse Spark Open appears relevant to the open-weight bonus; a useful multi-step workflow appears relevant to the agentic bonus. Check the detailed conditions before claiming either.
6. Plan the three-minute video early and verify public repository access while logged out.
