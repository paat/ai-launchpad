# Q — Phone calls through the owner's own assistant

Documentation research, 1 November 2026. One Grok 4.7 research leg, then the orchestrator re-opened the load-bearing sources (marked **re-opened** below). No account was created, no number was bought and no call was placed. No connection was runtime-tested.

## Question

Which AI services can answer a business's phone calls and make calls for it, so that the owner's own assistant (Claude, ChatGPT) runs them? Jobs in scope: answer while the owner is busy and take a message or book a slot; remind a customer about an overdue invoice; confirm a delivery with a supplier or courier; confirm or move an appointment; return a missed call and summarise it.

## Answer

- **The owner's Claude can already place calls with no Launchpad code.** Four services have a remote MCP with OAuth sign-in that can dial: **Retell** (Claude directory), **Speko** (Claude directory), **Bland** and **Telnyx** (custom connector URL). Vapi, AgentPhone and Retell's API server can dial too, but they take a pasted API key.
- **Retell's Claude connector is the closest fit to our approval rule.** Placing a call returns a preview first. The confirmation covers only that exact request, works once and expires after 2 minutes. There is a cap of 20 outbound calls per hour. Retell also says the connector cannot tell whether the owner or Claude sent the confirmation. So it does not replace Launchpad's own approval.
- **No vendor holds a call until the owner approves the exact number, purpose and script.** Launchpad needs a call approval kind, built like `send_message`: the assistant shows the number, the purpose and the opening lines, the owner says yes, then the assistant dials with its own connector and records the outcome.
- **Estonia is the weak spot.** An Estonian (+372) number comes from Twilio or Telnyx, with company documents. Twilio's review takes up to a few business days. Telnyx local numbers take about 72 hours and need an Estonian address. Estonian speech is solid only in ElevenLabs (`et`) and Azure (`et-EE`). Retell and Bland do not list Estonian. For the 4 Nov demo, call a team phone in English, or in Estonian through ElevenLabs. Do not buy a +372 number.
- **Two rules bind every call.** AI Act Art. 50 has applied since 2 Aug 2026: the agent says it is an AI at the start of the call. Estonian Electronic Communications Act § 103¹ requires prior consent before direct marketing to a person. An invoice reminder, delivery check or appointment confirmation is a service call, not marketing, as long as it offers nothing new.

This applies to the jobs above as follows. A reminder, delivery check or appointment confirmation about an existing deal is a service call. A call that offers something new is direct marketing and needs prior consent from a person. Every call opens with who is calling, that this is an AI assistant, whether the call is recorded, and how to reach a person. This memo is research, not legal advice.

## How Launchpad fits

Principle 6 (reuse existing MCP servers) applies, and the existing approval flow already covers this case:

1. **Catalog, not a new dialler.** Add catalog rows for Retell, Speko, Bland, Telnyx, ElevenLabs and Vapi so `connect_my_services` can suggest them in plain words ("so your assistant can call people for you"). Twilio is a carrier, not an assistant connection.
2. **A call approval kind, the same shape as `send_message`.** Recipient = the exact phone number in international format. Content = purpose plus the opening lines, including the AI notice. Limit = maximum minutes. The owner says yes in the chat (`owner_confirmation`), the assistant dials with its own connector and records the result: answered, no answer, voicemail or failed, plus a short summary. As with e-mail today, Launchpad records the approval and the outcome. It does not dial and cannot stop a connector that ignores it. A Launchpad-held dialler that enforces the digest is a later step, the same open question as for e-mail.
3. **Inbound is a standing rule, not a per-call approval.** The caller is already on the line. The owner sets what the agent may do (take a message, offer a time from the calendar, never promise money or discounts), and the assistant later reads the call summaries.
4. **Demo by 4 Nov:** Retell's Claude connector calls a team phone. The owner sees the preview, approves it once in Claude, and the call opens with the AI notice. No real customers and no +372 purchase.
