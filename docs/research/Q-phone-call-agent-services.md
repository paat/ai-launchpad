# Q — Phone calls through the owner's own assistant

Documentation research, 1 November 2026. One Grok 4.7 research leg, then the orchestrator re-opened the load-bearing sources (marked **re-opened** below). No account was created, no number was bought and no call was placed. No connection was runtime-tested.

## Question

Which AI services can answer a business's phone calls and make calls for it, so that the owner's own assistant (Claude, ChatGPT) runs them? Jobs in scope: answer while the owner is busy and take a message or book a slot; remind a customer about an overdue invoice; confirm a delivery with a supplier or courier; confirm or move an appointment; return a missed call and summarise it.

## Answer

- **The owner's Claude can already place calls with no Launchpad code.** Five services have a remote MCP with OAuth sign-in that can dial: **Retell** (Claude directory and a ChatGPT app), **Speko** (Claude directory), **Bland**, **Telnyx** and **AgentPhone** (custom connector URL). Vapi and Retell's separate API server can dial too, but they take a pasted API key.
- **Retell's Claude connector is the closest fit to our approval rule.** Placing a call returns a preview first. The confirmation covers only that exact request, works once and expires after 2 minutes. There is a cap of 20 outbound calls per hour. Retell also says the connector cannot tell whether the owner or Claude sent the confirmation. So it does not replace Launchpad's own approval.
- **No vendor holds a call until the owner approves the exact number, purpose and script.** Launchpad's `send_message` approval is the pattern to extend for calls. That is a follow-up: Launchpad does not implement phone-call logic now (owner, 1 Nov 2026).
- **Estonia is the weak spot.** An Estonian (+372) number comes from Twilio or Telnyx, with company documents. Twilio's review takes up to a few business days. Telnyx local numbers take about 72 hours and need an Estonian address. ElevenLabs (`et`) and Azure (`et-EE`) document Estonian; how either sounds on a phone line is untested. Retell and Bland do not list Estonian. Do not buy a +372 number for the hackathon.
- **Two rules bind every call.** AI Act Art. 50 has applied since 2 Aug 2026: the agent says it is an AI at the start of the call. Estonian Electronic Communications Act § 103¹ requires prior consent before direct marketing to a person, but § 103¹(6) excludes a real-time voice call. Whether an AI-voiced call counts as one is UNKNOWN. Policy default: a promotional call to a person needs their prior consent anyway. An invoice reminder, delivery check or appointment confirmation is a service call, not marketing, as long as it offers nothing new.

## Rules

| Rule | Tier | Source |
|---|---|---|
| AI Act Art. 50(1): "Providers shall ensure that AI systems intended to interact directly with natural persons are designed and developed in such a way that the natural persons concerned are informed that they are interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious from the point of view of a natural person who is reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect …" **Re-opened.** | A | [Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/) · [ELI](http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj) |
| Commission FAQ (updated 24 July 2026): "Article 50 of the AI Act applies as from 2 August 2026." People must be told "from the start of the first interaction". The Digital Omnibus did not delay Art. 50. **Re-opened.** | B | [FAQ](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/transparency-obligations-under-article-50-ai-act) |
| Electronic Communications Act § 103¹(1): direct marketing to a natural person's electronic contact data "on lubatud üksnes tema eelneval nõusolekul" (only with prior consent), meeting GDPR. § 103¹(2): to a legal person, allowed if each contact offers a free, easy opt-out. § 103¹(6): "Käesolevas paragrahvis sätestatut ei kohaldata reaalajas toimuvale mitmepoolsele häälkõnele" (this section does not apply to a real-time multi-party voice call). § 2 p 8¹: electronic contact data are data that let information be sent to a person over an electronic communications network, "sealhulgas" (including) fax, e-mail and SMS/MMS. Read in the version in force 01.10.2026–31.10.2026 (RT I, 11.07.2026, 85); the version in force from 1 Nov 2026 was not re-read. **Re-opened.** | A | [Riigi Teataja](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/public-api/api/v1/akt/111072026085/blob-html) |
| ePrivacy Directive Art. 13(1): automatic calling machines for direct marketing need prior consent. | A | [Directive 2002/58/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32002L0058), leg-only |
| AKI: a voice is personal data; silence or staying on the line after a warning is not consent. Written for public bodies; a private business may rely on legitimate interest after a balancing test. The business is the controller and the voice vendor is its processor (GDPR Art. 28). | B | [AKI](https://www.aki.ee/meist/teadlikkus/telefonikonede-seire), leg-only · [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679) |

This applies to the jobs above as follows. A reminder, delivery check or appointment confirmation about an existing deal is a service call. A call that offers something new is direct marketing. Because of § 103¹(6), the Estonian Act may not require consent for it; ePrivacy Art. 13 does for automatic calling machines, and an AI agent without a human on the line may be one. Until that is settled, treat a promotional call to a person as needing their prior consent. Every call opens with who is calling, that this is an AI assistant, whether the call is recorded, and how to reach a person. This memo is research, not legal advice.

## How Launchpad fits

Owner decision (1 Nov 2026): Launchpad does not implement phone-call logic now. What lands now is data:

1. **Catalogue rows, not a dialler.** Rows for the services above, so `connect_my_services` can suggest them in plain words ("so your assistant can call people for you"). Twilio and Azure Speech are listed as APIs, not assistant connections ([#135](https://github.com/paat/ai-launchpad/issues/135)).
2. **An Estonian-language badge** on every catalogue service whose own voice or text reaches people, because that is where Estonian matters ([#135](https://github.com/paat/ai-launchpad/issues/135)).

Follow-up, not built now:

- **A call approval kind** ([#136](https://github.com/paat/ai-launchpad/issues/136)), extending the `send_message` pattern. The approval binds the exact phone number in international format, the purpose and the opening lines (including the AI notice), and a maximum duration. The outcome is answered, no answer, voicemail or failed, plus a short summary. Today's approval schema has no duration field and outcomes have no summary, so both need adding. As with e-mail, Launchpad would record the approval and the outcome, not dial.
- **Inbound as a standing rule.** The caller is already on the line, so the owner sets what the agent may do (take a message, offer a time from the calendar, never promise money or discounts).
- **A demo** ([#136](https://github.com/paat/ai-launchpad/issues/136)) with Retell's Claude connector calling a team phone, with no real customers and no +372 purchase.
